# ECOS-Q12: Ecosystem Intervention Decision

**Status:** Complete

**Area:** Program closeout

**Depends on:** ECOS-Q01 through ECOS-Q11

## Question

For each verified ecosystem gap, should FERRIUM document, adapt, standardize,
contribute upstream, steward, prototype, or defer?

## Starting hypothesis

Most gaps will favor evidence, compatibility tests, adapters, or upstream
stewardship over new foundational crates or a curated distribution.

## Decision informed

Close the Crates Series, define the OSPREY ecosystem model, and decide whether
any bounded ecosystem capability advances.

## Outcome

Close the Crates Series by adopting the product-neutral Crate Ecosystem Ledger
and evidence-adapter vocabulary, routing contributions and stewardship through
current upstream owners, and advancing one read-only ecosystem-ledger/profile
diff as a future held-out proof candidate. Reject a FERRIUM distribution,
certification, universal score, automatic dependency or environment mutation,
and new foundational crates without a named material gap.

The Crates Series prerequisite for OSPREY specification work is satisfied.
Implementation remains unauthorized pending the remaining specifications,
held-out workflow, conformance, adoption, rollback, and separately approved
implementation pulse.

[Rust ecosystem intervention decisions](../../2026-08-10-rust-ecosystem-intervention-decisions.md).

All nine FERRIUM roles.
