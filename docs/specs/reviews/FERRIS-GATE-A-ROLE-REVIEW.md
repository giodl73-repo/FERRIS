# Ferris Gate A Nine-Role Review

Date: 2026-08-10
Specifications: CONTRACT-001, PLATFORM-001, APPLICATION-001
Gate foundation: PRODUCT-001 and GOVERNANCE-001
Disposition: Accepted as Draft after required revisions
Implementation authority: None

## Review question

Do the contract, profile, and application specifications preserve Rust and
external-owner semantics, support renewable enterprise commitments, and give
later scope and planning work a deterministic foundation without creating a
distribution, stable Rust ABI, hidden authority, or product lock-in?

## Executive disposition

All nine roles accept CONTRACT-001, PLATFORM-001, and APPLICATION-001 as Draft.
PRODUCT-001 retains its public-contract review disposition. GOVERNANCE-001
retains its Microsoft integration review disposition. Together, the five
specifications close the Draft form of Gate A without granting implementation
authority.

The review accepts:

- layered source, semantic, ABI, component, wire, and projection contracts;
- Typebook remaining product-neutral and separate;
- directional, scoped, non-Boolean compatibility;
- renewable profiles over exact selections, closures, environments, stages,
  assurance, support, expiry, removal, and rollback;
- separate Application Definition, Blueprint Model, and Application Contract
  records;
- Cargo retaining resolution and build authority;
- deterministic normalization with visible conflicts and unknowns;
- immutable roots and typed refs; and
- ordinary owner workflows remaining available after Ferris removal.

Proposed status is withheld pending executable fixtures and frozen schemas.

## Role dispositions

### Rust Safety Steward

Accept after requiring ownership, lifetime, aliasing, allocation, concurrency,
panic, unwind, unsafe, and toolchain evidence per applicable boundary. Build,
API, schema, and profile success cannot establish safety or soundness.

### Compiler Performance Engineer

Accept provided profile and application records retain active versus lock
closures, activities, targets, toolchains, build scripts, macros, native work,
and validation stages without turning contract metadata into performance
claims.

### Interop Boundary Auditor

Accept after separating semantic meaning, ABI mechanics, generated projection,
runtime component behavior, wire compatibility, and deployment. Requires
negative and migration fixtures for every boundary.

### AI Assurance Skeptic

Accept after making AI proposals non-authoritative and preserving source,
evidence, confidence, unknowns, policy, approval, and failure. No generated
default may reduce work or expand support.

### Ecosystem Strategist

Accept because Typebook remains independent, profiles avoid distribution and
certification, Cargo remains authoritative, and upstream owners retain their
contracts and servicing.

### Rust Maintainer

Accept if diagnostics use crate, feature, target, operation, profile, contract,
provider, and failed-stage vocabulary and if removal restores ordinary Cargo
without hidden state.

### Native Platform Adopter

Accept for Draft. Proposed status requires executed Windows and Unix native
boundaries, explicit ABI and tool requirements, support contacts, deployment,
recovery, substitution, and rollback.

### Scope Keeper

Accept after preserving the three-record rule and preventing CONTRACT-001,
PLATFORM-001, or APPLICATION-001 from absorbing SCOPE, PLANNING, EXECUTION,
GOVERNANCE, or CONFORMANCE responsibilities.

### Validation Checker

Accept as Draft. Proposed status requires fixed schemas, exact repositories,
commands, positive, negative, failure, unsupported, stale, version-skew,
migration, renewal, substitution, rollback, and removal fixtures.

## Required revisions completed

The specifications now require:

- explicit layered identity and authority;
- complete operation semantics;
- projection-loss records;
- stage-specific profile results;
- exact lock and target-active closures;
- support, servicing, expiry, renewal, substitution, removal, and rollback;
- deterministic application normalization;
- conflict and AI-authority rules;
- immutable roots and typed refs;
- multi-state rather than Boolean outcomes; and
- ordinary owner-workflow preservation.

## Remaining gates

Before Proposed status:

1. freeze canonical schemas and projections;
2. select Typebook/RUNE contract versions;
3. execute one Rust, Typebook, C ABI, WIT, and wire/data projection family;
4. freeze at least six profile fixtures and three application shapes;
5. execute cross-platform, native, migration, renewal, substitution, rollback,
   and removal controls;
6. define exact diagnostics and machine outputs; and
7. repeat all nine role reviews over measured results.

## Decision

Advance CONTRACT-001, PLATFORM-001, and APPLICATION-001 to Draft.

Do not authorize implementation.
