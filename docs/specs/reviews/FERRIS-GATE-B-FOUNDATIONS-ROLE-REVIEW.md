# Ferris Gate B Foundations Nine-Role Review

Date: 2026-08-10
Specifications: SCOPE-001, FOREST-002, IDENTITY-001, EVIDENCE-001, FOREST-003
Disposition: Accepted as Draft after required revisions
Implementation authority: None

## Review question

Do the first Gate B specifications define a deterministic, product-neutral
planning-truth foundation without collapsing scope, identity, owner evidence,
projection, trust, compatibility, or execution into one graph or authority?

## Executive disposition

All nine roles accept the five specifications as Draft.

The review accepts:

- multi-dimensional scope coordinates and directional mappings;
- owner-native anchors and conservative widening;
- deterministic canonical Forest records and immutable roots;
- distinct identity domains, typed refs, and generation history;
- read-only, one-owner evidence adapters;
- reproducible maps and ledgers over immutable snapshots; and
- explicit unknown, unsupported, stale, unavailable, failed, and
  not-observed states.

Proposed status and implementation remain withheld pending frozen schemas,
portable fixtures, cross-platform execution, and independent projection
reproduction.

## Role dispositions

### Rust Safety Steward

Accept after requiring scope and evidence to preserve unsafe, ownership,
lifetime, allocation, panic, unwind, concurrency, macro, build-script, and
native boundaries. Narrowing or aggregation cannot erase a safety obligation.

### Compiler Performance Engineer

Accept provided fine-grained compiler evidence remains version-coupled,
budgeted, optional, and replaceable. Scope collection cost and selected versus
full-reference work must be measured before performance claims.

### Interop Boundary Auditor

Accept after preserving distinct source, semantic, ABI, component, wire,
native, generated-binding, runtime, and deployment identities. Projections may
join these records but cannot claim equivalence from shape.

### AI Assurance Skeptic

Accept because AI may propose mappings or explanations but cannot establish
owner facts, narrow mandatory work, relabel inference as observation, move
refs, approve policy, or convert predictions into evidence.

### Ecosystem Strategist

Accept because adapters retain owner boundaries, the canonical model remains
product-neutral, extensions are removable, and owner interface gaps route
through Ecosystem Bridge rather than permanent Ferris capture.

### Rust Maintainer

Accept if diagnostics use owner vocabulary and explain selected, omitted,
widened, stale, unsupported, failed, and unknown scope without requiring graph
terminology. Cargo and repository-native workflows must remain sufficient.

### Native Platform Adopter

Accept for Draft. Proposed status requires Windows and Unix fixtures covering
host/target tools, SDKs, linkers, native artifacts, runtime loading,
permissions, path behavior, failure, cleanup, and removal.

### Scope Keeper

Accept after retaining separate SCOPE, FOREST, IDENTITY, EVIDENCE, CAUSALITY,
PLANNING, TRUST, and EXECUTION responsibilities. The five specifications do
not authorize planning, prediction, resolution, mutation, or trust decisions.

### Validation Checker

Accept as Draft. Proposed status requires canonical schemas, malformed and
version-skew fixtures, every mapping cardinality, every ref type, adapter
failure matrices, deterministic serialization, consistency violations,
independent projections, and complete removal.

## Required revisions completed

The specifications now require:

- typed axes with explicit omitted and unknown semantics;
- directional, cardinality-aware, conditional mappings;
- owner-native anchors and safe widening;
- scope-detail economics budgets;
- separate claim classes and identity domains;
- canonical deterministic serialization without secrets;
- immutable roots and compare-and-set ref generations;
- read-only adapter defaults and side-effect classification;
- source traceability for every projected claim;
- snapshot isolation and cross-projection consistency; and
- failure-shaped rather than success-shaped partial results.

## Remaining gates

Before Proposed status:

1. freeze canonical schemas and extension rules;
2. build exact cross-command and cross-platform scope fixtures;
3. implement malformed, unsupported, stale, permission, conflict, and
   version-skew adapter controls;
4. execute concurrent ref, retention, collection, and rollback cases;
5. reproduce required maps and ledgers with two projection implementations;
6. measure fine-grained evidence cost and fallback economics;
7. demonstrate complete removal with ordinary Cargo and owner tools; and
8. repeat all nine role reviews over measured results.

## Decision

Advance SCOPE-001, FOREST-002, IDENTITY-001, EVIDENCE-001, and FOREST-003 to
Draft.

Do not authorize implementation.
