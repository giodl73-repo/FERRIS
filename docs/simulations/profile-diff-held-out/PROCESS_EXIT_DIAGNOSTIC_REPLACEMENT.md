# Independent Process-Exit Diagnostic Replacement Contract

Status: Authorized; unexecuted
Program: `FERRIS-P24-INDEPENDENT-PROCESS-EXIT-DIAGNOSTIC-REPLACEMENT`
Schema: `ferris.process-exit-diagnostic-replacement/v1`
Disclosure tier: `sanitized-reproducer` (precommitted before generation)

## Purpose

This contract authorizes one new independent diagnostic search for the
released public category `process-exit-agreement`.

Pulse 24 is a replacement program. It is not a retry, resume, reseed,
rescore, replay, continuation, or reuse of Pulse 22. Pulse 22 remains
permanently `invalid`, permanently non-retryable, and unable to produce any
category conclusion.

This program is diagnostic only. It is not certification, a held-out score,
a Pulse 17 retry, or product-fix authority. The valid Pulse 17 failure remains
immutable regardless of the Pulse 24 outcome.

## Mandatory synthetic preflight

After digest verification and before any candidate launch, custody MUST run
at least two harmless synthetic atomic cross-platform pairs through the exact
qualified collector.

Each preflight pair MUST:

- use fixed harmless synthetic commands and payloads;
- retain one Windows and one Ubuntu record;
- durably synchronize each process record;
- seal the pair only after both records exist;
- verify the sealed pair by a fresh read-only process; and
- leave zero interrupted-write residue.

Preflight rows are infrastructure controls, not candidates. They do not count
toward the 512-case/platform or 1,024-process search bounds and cannot produce
a `process-exit-agreement` conclusion.

Any preflight launch, stream, durability, reload, residue, seal, digest, or
cardinality failure makes the Pulse 24 package `invalid` before candidates.
No candidate may launch after such a failure. Repair or another preflight
attempt requires a separately authorized later pulse.

## Transactional pair collection

Every reached candidate is one transaction:

1. directly launch the immutable Ferris binary once on the first platform;
2. durably synchronize that complete process record;
3. directly launch once on the partner platform;
4. durably synchronize that complete process record;
5. create and durably synchronize the pair seal only after both records
   exist;
6. start a fresh read-only process;
7. reload the two records and pair seal from durable storage;
8. verify identity, cardinality, stream digests, exits, and seal joins; and
9. only then classify the completed pair.

An in-memory row, unsynchronized row, partial pair, or same-process reread is
not a completed pair and MUST NOT be classified.

After the first candidate launch, any launch, timeout, stream-read,
stream-bound, durability, residue, pair-seal, fresh-process reload,
cardinality, manifest, classifier, or custody failure invalidates the entire
package and permanently prohibits further launches under Pulse 24. It cannot
be retried or converted into `no-reproduction`.

## Reproduction and minimization

The target category is reproduced only by a completed transactional
cross-platform pair for which at least one incorporated public target
predicate is false.

After reproduction, custody MUST preserve the first pair and stop search
launches. Minimization is a separately identified phase bounded to 128
transformations and 256 processes, with at most one launch per derived
candidate per platform and zero retries. Complete lineage is mandatory.

A completed reproduction publishes a fresh harmless public reproducer and a
valid `ferris.post-score-diagnostic-release/v1` receipt. The receipt's legacy
`score_attempt` and `scorer_attempt` fields remain `1` for schema
compatibility and refer only to Pulse 24's one immutable diagnostic
disposition.
