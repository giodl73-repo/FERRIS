# Wave: Owner-Native Focused Validation

Status: Complete; Pulse 01 accepted and closed

## Product outcome

Let a Ferris user see whether a selected opaque owner entrypoint is declared
focused, subsystem-wide, or comprehensive and which owner preparation procedure
and working directory apply, without Ferris interpreting or executing either.

## Research basis

The decision is grounded in
[`Owner-Native Focused Validation`](../../../docs/research/2026-09-11-owner-native-focused-validation.md),
including `FERRIS-FOCUSED-001` through `FERRIS-FOCUSED-005`.

## Classification

One bounded, local, read-only validation-planning wave.

## Budget

- exactly one production pulse;
- one v2 development fixture;
- focused core, CLI, schema, documentation, role, and diff validation;
- at most one localized corrective pass; and
- no adopter migration, hosted execution, benchmarking, or successor pulse.

## Completion condition

- v1 and no-contract behavior remain unchanged;
- one closed v2 contract carries explicit breadth, opaque preparation identity,
  and a normalized workspace-relative working directory;
- selected v2 metadata is present in JSON identity and human explanation;
- negative controls reject unsafe or ambiguous declarations;
- no command interpretation or execution is introduced; and
- the targeted validation and role review pass.

## Abandonment condition

Stop if the slice requires parsing command text, recipes, workflow files, or
environment values; executing preparation or validation; changing Cargo
selection; introducing another architectural layer; or altering an adopter.

## Next product priority

After this pulse, return to the separately governed enterprise onboarding
decision. Cost and duplicate-work observations require their own evidence and
authority.
