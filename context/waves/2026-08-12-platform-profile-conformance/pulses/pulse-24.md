# Pulse 24: Independent Process-Exit Diagnostic Replacement Authority

Status: Authorized; unexecuted
Implementation authority: Public governance, machine-readable declaration,
nine-role review, and test-only validation only

## Goal

Authorize one new independent replacement diagnostic search for the released
public category `process-exit-agreement`.

Pulse 24 is not a Pulse 22 retry, resume, reseed, rescore, replay,
continuation, or reuse. Pulse 22 remains permanently `invalid` and
non-retryable with zero completed pairs and no category conclusion.

## Fixed execution target

Any later custody execution is bound to immutable Ferris cutoff
`cef0daabc349ac2333869959f21b9a3106e10484`.

The qualified collector may be reused only by exact pinned source digest.
Before candidates, the new custodian must independently verify the public
report, source, test, Windows-environment, and Ubuntu-environment digests and
pass at least two harmless synthetic atomic cross-platform preflight pairs.
Preflights are not candidates. A preflight failure invalidates before
candidates and may be repaired only by a separately authorized later pulse.

## New custody boundary

Pulse 24 requires a new custody identity and workspace, new private
deterministic seed and commitment, new independently frozen classifier,
generator, case and coverage manifest, and a new fresh corpus.

Access to, reuse of, correlation with, or inference from Pulse 17 private
material, Pulse 22 private material, or Pulse 19 case bytes is prohibited.
Implementation authors may not construct, select, inspect, or execute
candidates.

## Search and collection bounds

- maximum 512 unique fresh cases per platform;
- maximum 1,024 candidate search processes;
- one launch per reached candidate and platform;
- zero candidate retries and one search execution;
- stop after the first completed cross-platform pair reproducing
  `process-exit-agreement`;
- durably synchronize each process record;
- seal a pair only after both platform records exist;
- verify the pair through a fresh-process reload before classification; and
- invalidate the entire package and prohibit further launches after any
  post-first-candidate launch, stream, durability, seal, reload, manifest, or
  cardinality failure.

The complete Pulse 22 public generation domains, mandatory interactions,
independent oracle, and target predicates are preserved by exact public
contract incorporation and exact machine-readable equality checks.

## Result boundary

The disclosure tier is precommitted to `sanitized-reproducer`.

If reproduced, minimization is separate and bounded to 128 transformations
and 256 processes. Publication requires a fresh harmless reproducer and valid
`ferris.post-score-diagnostic-release/v1` receipt.

If not reproduced, only bounded aggregate coverage may be published. If
invalid, the public result must contain the exact public-safe blocker and
counts with a null category conclusion.

No result is a score, certification, support claim, product-fix authority, or
PLATFORM-001 status change.

## Stop conditions

Stop rather than widen this pulse if work would:

- execute a preflight or candidate in this governance change;
- change production source or dependencies;
- access or derive from Pulse 17, Pulse 22 private material, or Pulse 19 case
  bytes;
- change the immutable cutoff or any qualified collector digest;
- patch or requalify the collector;
- weaken mandatory public coverage or an oracle predicate;
- retry a candidate or continue after a package-invalidating failure;
- publish a reproduction without bounded minimization and a valid receipt; or
- change PLATFORM-001 from Draft.
