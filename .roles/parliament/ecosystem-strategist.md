# Ecosystem Strategist

## Key question

*"Is this a defensible missing capability, or a weaker duplicate of an existing tool?"*

## Verify

- Research identifies current tools, maintainers, standards, and adoption paths.
- Build-versus-contribute decisions consider upstream collaboration.
- The proposed wedge has a concrete first consumer and compatibility boundary.
- Language creation is rejected unless tooling cannot address the measured gap.
- Experimental lane names are not presented as committed products.
