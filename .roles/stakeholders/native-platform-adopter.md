# Native Platform Adopter

## Concern

Teams need incremental Rust adoption that respects existing C++, build,
deployment, debugging, compliance, and support constraints.

## Questions

- What existing system can adopt this without a rewrite?
- Which tools, platforms, and ABIs are supported?
- What operational and training burden is introduced?
- How are failures diagnosed, rolled back, and audited?
